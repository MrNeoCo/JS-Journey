# Hardware – Speaking Notes

---

---

## Top Interview Questions

**Q. Laptop not turning on?**
Verify power, adapter, battery, POST, run diagnostics, escalate if hardware failure.

**Q. HDD vs SSD?**
SSD is faster, more reliable, and uses flash memory. HDD uses spinning disks.

**Q. Hardware or Software issue?**
Check symptoms, isolate the cause, troubleshoot accordingly.

---

## Keywords

IP Address • DNS • DHCP • Gateway • VPN • Ping • Troubleshoot • Resolve

---

## Top Interview Questions

**Q. Internet is not working. What will you do?**

Check cable/Wi-Fi → Verify IP (`ipconfig`) → Ping Gateway → Ping Google → Check DNS (`nslookup`) → Flush DNS → Renew IP → Restart adapter.

---

**Q. DNS vs DHCP?**

**DNS:** Converts domain names into IP addresses.

**DHCP:** Automatically assigns IP addresses to devices.

---

**Q. What is VPN?**

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) creates a secure, encrypted connection to access a private network over the internet.

---

**Q. Which networking commands do you use?**

`ipconfig`, `ping`, `tracert`, `nslookup`, `netstat`

## Top Interview Questions

**Q. What is Active Directory?**

Active Directory (AD) is Microsoft's directory service used to manage users, computers, groups, and resources in a domain.

---

**Q. User forgot the password. What will you do?**

Verify identity → Reset password → Unlock account (if locked) → Ask the user to sign in and change the password.

---

**Q. What is an OU?**

An Organizational Unit (OU) is a container used to organize users, computers, and groups for easier management and Group Policy application.

---

**Q. What is a Domain Controller?**

A Domain Controller (DC) is a server that authenticates users and manages Active Directory within a domain.

---

**Q. What is Group Policy?**

Group Policy is used to centrally manage security settings, user configurations, and computer settings across a domain.

---

After confirming the issue is resolved, answering the user's questions, and ensuring they can continue working normally.

# Mock Interviews – Speaking Notes

## How to Answer Technical Questions

Follow this structure:

**Understand → Explain → Troubleshoot → Verify → Escalate (if required)**

---

## Professional Opening

"Certainly."

"Based on my understanding..."

"My approach would be..."

"First, I would verify the issue..."

---

## While Explaining

"After verifying the issue, I would isolate the root cause."

"Then I would perform the required troubleshooting."

"Finally, I would confirm the issue is resolved with the user."

---

## If You Don't Know

"I'm not completely sure, but based on my understanding..."

"I would verify the information before making any changes."

> Never say **"I don't know."**

---

## Closing an Answer

"Once the issue is resolved, I'll verify it with the user, document the work performed, and close the ticket."

---

## Golden Interview Formula

```text
Understand
      ↓
Verify
      ↓
Troubleshoot
      ↓
Resolve
      ↓
Verify with User
      ↓
Document & Close
```

---

## Interview Tips

- Think for 2–3 seconds before answering.
- Speak slowly and confidently.
- Keep answers structured.
- Don't rush.
- Always mention **verification** and **user confirmation**.

---

Introduce yourself → Experience → Skills → Why you're applying.

---

**Q. How do you troubleshoot a user's issue?**

Verify → Gather information → Troubleshoot → Resolve → Confirm → Document.

---

**Q. Why should we hire you?**

Highlight your technical skills, troubleshooting approach, communication skills, and willingness to learn.

# Spoken English – Speaking Notes

> **Goal:** Speak clearly, professionally, and confidently during interviews and user interactions.

---

# 1. Professional Opening Lines

Use these instead of casual words:

❌ "Tell me your problem."

✅ "Could you please explain the issue you are facing?"

---

❌ "Wait."

✅ "Please allow me a moment while I check this."

---

❌ "I will see."

✅ "I'll look into this and get back to you."

---

# 2. User Communication Phrases

## Understanding Issue

- "I understand the concern."
- "Let me help you with this issue."
- "Could you please provide more details?"

## While Troubleshooting

- "Let me perform a few checks."
- "I'll verify the settings first."
- "Based on my findings, I'll proceed with the next step."

## After Resolution

- "The issue has been resolved."
- "Could you please confirm from your end?"
- "Thank you for confirming."

---

# 3. Interview Speaking Templates

## Starting an Answer

- "Based on my experience..."
- "My approach would be..."
- "The first step I would take is..."

---

## Explaining Troubleshooting

Use:

"First, I would verify the issue. Then I would identify the root cause, perform the required troubleshooting steps, and finally confirm the resolution with the user."

---

## If You Don't Know an Answer

❌ "I don't know."

✅ "I don't have hands-on experience with this yet, but I understand the concept and I would like to learn more about it."

---
