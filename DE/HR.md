# HR Interview Questions

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Walk me through your resume.
3. Why do you want to join our company?
4. Why are you leaving your current job?
5. Why should we hire you?
6. What are your strengths?
7. What are your weaknesses?
8. Where do you see yourself in 3–5 years?
9. Tell me about a challenge you faced at work.
10. Describe a time you handled a difficult customer.
11. Tell me about a time you worked under pressure.
12. How do you prioritize multiple tasks?
13. Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned.
14. How do you handle criticism or feedback?
15. Have you ever disagreed with a teammate? How did you handle it?
16. Why do you want to work in Desktop Support?
17. Why do you want to move from your current company?
18. What motivates you?
19. What are your salary expectations?
20. Are you willing to work in shifts, weekends, or on-call?
21. Are you comfortable working from the office?
22. How do you handle stress?
23. What would your previous manager say about you?
24. What are your hobbies or interests?
25. Do you have any questions for us?
