To keep things systematic, every module will follow the exact same format:

Concept – What it is.

How It Works – Internal working explained simply.
Where You'll Use It – Real desktop support scenarios.
Hands-on Practice – Windows steps or commands to try.
Troubleshooting – Common problems and how to solve them.
Interview Questions – Most frequently asked questions.
Professional Answers – How to answer confidently in English.
Quick Revision Notes – One-page summary.
